I have always wanted to be able to read long-form text on the web like I would read a magazine or a book or even an e-reader. Read a column of text, move your eye to the next column, turn the page. Or swipe left and right to browse. It is a natural way to read. Endless vertical scrolling is just not as pleasant.

Unfortunately, I don't know enough about web standards to implement this. I only know what I'd like to see. But, since November, 2025 we have lived in a new time of wonder. We can ask LLMs to create things for us. Yes, you may call this vibe-coding but would you rather have something vibe-coded vs. nothing at all? In this case, I'll choose the vibes. You do you.
